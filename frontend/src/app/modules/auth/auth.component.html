Smart and Fair Employee Scheduling

Shiftly is a user-friendly online platform designed to simplify employee shift scheduling for businesses of all sizes. It allows managers to create and share weekly work rotas effortlessly, ensuring that shifts are assigned fairly and efficiently. Employees can access their schedules in real-time via the platform, promoting transparency and reducing scheduling conflicts. With features like employee invitations, iCalendar integration, and intuitive scheduling tools, Shiftly streamlines the rostering process, saving time and enhancing operational efficiency.
