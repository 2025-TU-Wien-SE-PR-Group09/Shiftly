Calendar Subscription

To display your Shyft shifts in an external calendar, first generate a token and then copy the link to your clipboard. Add your Shyft shifts to your external calendar using the link. Alternatively, you can also download the iCal file directly. However, please note that in this case, the appointments in your external calendar cannot be automatically updated.

If you believe someone has gained knowledge of your token, you can generate a new one at any time.

How to use:

  1. Copy the URL above
  2. Open your calendar application (Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar, etc.)
  3. Find the option to add a calendar by URL or subscribe to a calendar
  4. Paste the URL and save
  5. Your shifts will now appear in your calendar and update automatically

Alternative: Direct Download

You can also download the iCal file directly, but it won't update automatically in your calendar.
